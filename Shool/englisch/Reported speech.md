## A unusual Day 
Yesterday was a unusual day the lights gone out and our only light source was a flash light. We had to solve riddles to escape the city.
 
In the Morning I walked through the city. After some time I found a road that I never walked on before. I walked down the road that i didn´t know. It was a dead end and in the wall at the end of the road was something that looked like an Map or an pattern. The thing consisted of circles and crosses. I noted the thing down and went Home.

Some time after I went home the lights gone out and the siren stared to howl. In the trouble i found three other people. Those three other people were Serda, Nils and Michel. Michel had found a Book with some pictures in side with something we never saw. Serda that had a box that had a number code inside helped us to unlock a lock. After we opened the lock we found a longer letter that guided us to the forbidden place that would lead us to the surface.

While we walked to the forbidden place we found a riddle. The riddle consisted out of the three ways every way with a text above it.  Also in the letter that we found before stad that two ways would lie and one would be correct. After we sucsesfully solved the riddle we got to the end of the way.
Serda pulled the lever so we could see what was outside. The same time serda pulled the lever a door close an we couldn´t see Serda anymore but he could still hear us. We asked him what he saw an he tough us that he saw a bomb that was set to six minutes and was ticking down. He told us that there were many riddles that we needed to solve to defuse the bomb. The first riddle was to cut the wires in the right order. Michel told us that under the pictures some text stood. With the pictures knew in wich order we had to cut the wires and told that Serda. Serda told us after he cut the wires that the next riddle consisted out of some Hydrographs that needed to be pressed in the right order. Nils that found the note with the Hydrographs told him in wich order he needed to press the buttons. 
The last riddle he told us were many buttons. I gussed that it would be the thing that i found. And told him how he needed to press the buttons so we could all go outside. But it was too late and and the bomb exploded.
